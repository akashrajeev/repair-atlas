When an AI workflow fails, fixing one thing at a time can miss the real answer. Some failures only clear when two or three fixes land together, and some clear with any one of several fixes. Repair Atlas takes the fixes you already have, tries them in combinations, and shows the repair sets that work, with no smaller subset that also works. It marks which fixes are needed together and which are alternatives, then replays each result so you can see it pass and see the smaller parts fail. That saves guessing and re-running by hand.
